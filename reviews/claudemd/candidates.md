- [mobile] Entity detail page header pattern (Breadcrumbs + h1 + badges on left, action buttons on right) must use `flex flex-col gap-3 sm:flex-row sm:justify-between sm:items-center` with `min-w-0` on the title block and `flex-wrap` on both the inner title+badges row and the action row. Without it, long titles or many action buttons push the right side off the viewport on mobile. Applied to 7 detail pages and `src/components/PageHeader.tsx` (commit c8d8969).
- [mobile] iOS Safari auto-zooms when a focused `<input>`, `<textarea>`, or `<select>` has `font-size < 16px`. Fixed globally with a `@media (max-width: 640px)` rule in `src/app/globals.css` that forces form fields to 16px on phone widths (preserves pinch-zoom, unlike `maximum-scale=1`). Heads-up for future Tailwind classes: `text-sm` (14px) on form fields is overridden on mobile by design.

Pattern: report pages look up the matching dashboard from `dashboardChoices` to get the label.
